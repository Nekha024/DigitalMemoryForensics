01 / Forensic Evidence Intake

Every digital forensic investigation begins by establishing a chain of custody. Before files can be read or parsed, Memoir registers them under a specific Investigation Case.

🔒

Cryptographic Integrity

The system generates SHA-256 hashes of all uploads to ensure evidence cannot be altered after ingestion.

📋

Metadata Logging

02 / Text Extraction & Chunking

Memoir extracts raw text from PDF, DOCX, or text files. However, large documents cannot be parsed by LLM reasoning engines in one block due to token limitations and context noise.

We solve this by Semantic Chunking: splitting evidence into smaller overlapping sections (~150 tokens) to preserve contextual boundaries.

Interactive Sandbox Tip

Click on any generated chunk in the right panel to highlight the exact text section on the left where it was extracted.

03 / Vector Space Indexing

To find relevant facts semantically, Memoir converts text chunks into high-dimensional vector embeddings. Text snippets with similar meaning are mapped closer together in coordinate space.

We index these vectors in Qdrant. In this visualizer, you can explore the 2D projected representation of the Case Vector Space.
